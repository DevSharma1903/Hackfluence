Design a high-fidelity desktop SaaS application called "Zukunft AI".

Tagline:
Turning Signals Into Foresight

OVERVIEW

Zukunft AI is an AI-powered creator intelligence platform that helps content creators discover emerging audience interests and future content opportunities.

The system analyzes publicly available creator signals such as YouTube video metadata, audience discussions, and trend data to identify topics gaining momentum and recommend future content ideas.

IMPORTANT:

This is a realistic hackathon MVP.

The design should feel achievable by a small engineering team.

Avoid futuristic AI dashboards, fake enterprise products, excessive analytics, and unrealistic features.

The application should communicate:

"What should this creator create next?"

---

DESIGN STYLE

* Premium SaaS
* Dark mode
* Similar quality to Linear, Stripe, Notion, Perplexity, Vercel
* Clean typography
* Minimal color palette
* Professional
* Information-rich but not cluttered
* Modern startup feel
* No glassmorphism
* No neon cyberpunk effects
* No excessive gradients

---

NAVIGATION

Only 4 screens:

1. Analyze Channel
2. Dashboard
3. Topic Analysis
4. Recommendations

No login page.

No pricing page.

No settings page.

No agency dashboard.

No enterprise dashboard.

No market intelligence dashboard.

No future scope screens.

This is a focused MVP.

CRITICAL DESIGN RULES

Every recommendation must have evidence.

Every metric must be explainable.

Do not use:

Confidence Scores

Prediction Scores

AI Confidence

Future Prediction

Audience Migration

Private User Data

Platform Internal Data

The product should rely only on publicly available creator signals.

---

FINAL IMPRESSION

The final prototype should feel like:

"A realistic creator analytics product that helps creators discover future content opportunities using audience discussions and trend signals."

A judge should immediately believe:

* The problem is real.
* The MVP is buildable.
* The data sources are accessible.
* The recommendations are explainable.
* The team understands both the technical and business aspects of the product.
